Privacy Policy for Guanaco Labs

Effective Date: March 30, 2026

Welcome to Guanaco Labs. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and the integrity of your scientific data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our nanobody generation services and related platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies you personally, as well as scientific data necessary to provide our services.

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we collect standard details such as your name, email address, organization name, and billing information.
  • Scientific & Service Data: To generate nanobodies, we collect the antigen identities and antigen sequences you submit to our platform.
  • Guanaco Labs Generated Data: We independently generate sequencing data resulting from our llama immunizations based on your submissions.
  • Technical Data: We automatically collect basic log data (e.g., IP addresses, browser types) necessary for website functionality and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following operational and developmental purposes:

  • Providing Services: To execute the generation of nanobodies in llamas as a service and deliver the resulting data to you.
  • Algorithm Training: We use submitted antigen identities, sequences, and the resulting immunization data to train, test, and improve "Vicuna," our proprietary nanobody prediction algorithm.
  • Account Management: To process transactions, maintain your account, and provide customer support.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with essential third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • Hosting Services: Your data is stored and processed securely using Amazon Web Services (AWS).
  • Payment Processors: Transactions are handled by our secure, third-party payment processor, [Insert Payment Processor Name]. We do not store your full financial information on our servers.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We handle the retention and deletion of personal account data and scientific data differently to ensure your privacy while maintaining our research capabilities:

  • Personal Account Data: You may delete your account and associated personal data at any time via your account settings. We retain this personal data for a short-term buffer of 24 hours following your request to allow for account recovery, after which it is permanently deleted.
  • Antigen Sequences & Scientific Data: Unless explicitly requested, deleting your account does not automatically delete the antigen identities or sequences you have submitted, nor does it remove them from the Vicuna algorithm's training datasets.
  • Special Deletion Requests: If you require the deletion of your submitted antigen sequences, you must submit a special request by emailing help@guanacolabs.com.

5. International Data Transfers

Guanaco Labs is located in the United States. If you are accessing our services from Europe, Latin America, or other regions with laws governing data collection and use that may differ from U.S. law, please note that you are transferring your data to the United States. By using our services, you consent to this transfer and processing via AWS in the United States, safeguarded by standard contractual clauses or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & Global Standards)

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area), you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: You can request the deletion of your data (subject to the scientific data provisions outlined in Section 4).
  • Objection & Restriction: You can object to our processing of your data or request that we restrict its processing.
